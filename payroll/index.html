Can we engage you for recruitment only?

Yes. The concept service structure allows businesses to enquire about recruitment, payroll or both separately. Final service scope is confirmed during consultation.

Do you support payroll for small businesses?

Payroll outsourcing enquiries are welcome from businesses of different sizes. Requirements and suitability would be assessed before a proposal.

These are important Malaysian payroll considerations. The precise compliance services offered must be confirmed with the provider before any commercial claim or engagement.
